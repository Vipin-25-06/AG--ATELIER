Our Story

Clothing made with intention,
worn with purpose.

AG ATELIER was founded on a simple belief: that exceptional clothing should be accessible to everyone who values quality over quantity.

Founded 2018

A wardrobe built on fewer, better pieces.

AG ATELIER started as a reaction to fast fashion — the endless churn of cheap trends that fall apart after a single season. We wanted to create a different kind of clothing brand: one that obsesses over fabric, fit, and longevity.

Our first collection of six essential pieces sold out in 48 hours. Since then, we've remained deliberately small, releasing only 2-3 collections per year to ensure every piece is exactly right.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🧵

Uncompromising Quality

Every fabric is sourced directly from mills we've vetted in person. We reject up to 30% of fabric rolls that don't meet our standards.

🌱

Sustainable Practice

We use organic and recycled materials wherever possible. Our packaging is 100% recycled and our manufacturing partners are fair-trade certified.

✏️

Timeless Design

We don't chase trends. Every piece is designed to be worn for years, becoming better with age as it takes on the shape of its wearer.
